Esports Highlight Reels

Terms of Service

Effective September 4, 2026 Operator single individual Users operator only

Esports Highlight Reels is a personal automation tool, not a public product. It has exactly one user: the individual who operates it and owns the connected accounts below. These terms describe what the tool does and don't apply to anyone else.

1

What this is

A self-hosted n8n workflow that reads publicly available esports tournament broadcasts on YouTube, uses an AI model to identify highlight-worthy moments, automatically reframes and trims a short vertical clip, and — only after the operator manually approves that specific clip — republishes it to the operator's own accounts on:

  • YouTube
  • Facebook
  • TikTok
  • Instagram
2

Human approval, every time

No clip is ever published automatically. Each candidate is sent to the operator as a message with the clip attached; publishing happens only after an explicit approval on that message. Rejected or unanswered clips are discarded and never posted.

3

Source material

Source footage is drawn from official, publicly published tournament broadcasts. The tool does not download or republish anyone's private content, and does not scrape or interact with any TikTok user's account other than the operator's own.

4

No public accounts, no signup

This tool offers no registration, login, or account creation to third parties, has no public-facing pages, and is not offered as a service to anyone besides its operator.

5

Changes

Because this is a single-operator tool rather than a public service, these terms change only when the tool's own behavior changes, and the "Effective" date above is updated accordingly.

Questions about this integration — including for TikTok's app review — go to esportshighlightbot@gmail.com.
